A custom system or off-the-shelf software

SHEET 03 / 06 · CHOICE

Off-the-shelf software is enough when

  • the process is standard and works the same way in hundreds of companies: accounting, email, a single shop’s checkout;
  • you need it tomorrow, with no changes;
  • a subscription costs less than building and supporting your own system.

Custom development pays off when

  • the process is your advantage, and packaged software forces you to break it;
  • data lives in several systems and people move it by hand;
  • you have hit the limits of a packaged product or its licence;
  • you need to own the code and not depend on a single vendor.

Often the best answer is in between: packaged software with integrations and extensions around it. If that turns out cheaper, we will say so at the estimate stage.

Frequently asked questions

SHEET 05 / 06 · QUESTIONS
How much does development cost?

It depends on scope and complexity, so there is no single price. Describe the task and within 48 hours we return a work plan with an estimate. Price and timeline are fixed before development starts.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on scope and are also fixed in the work plan. We show the work at a demo every two weeks, so progress is visible from the first sprint.

Who owns the code?

You do. With the acceptance act we hand over the code, tests and documentation — 100 % your property.

Can you take over a project from another vendor?

Yes. We start with an audit of the code, architecture and infrastructure, document the system, then develop it further or move it to modern architecture step by step.

Do you only work with companies in Tbilisi?

No. We are based in Tbilisi and also work with companies in other cities and countries: calls, demos and documents happen online.

How do we start?

Write to us: what your company does, what task needs solving and which systems you already use. We sign an NDA before discussing details.
